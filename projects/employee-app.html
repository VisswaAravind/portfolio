Overview

Digitizing Workforce Operations

The Petal Employee App is a centralized employee management solution designed to digitize workforce operations. Many organizations rely on manual systems to track attendance, manage leave requests, and monitor employee tasks, which often leads to inaccurate records and increased administrative workload.

Petal Employee solves this problem by combining an RFID-based attendance system with a mobile application. The system enables quick and accurate attendance capture while allowing employers and employees to manage leave requests, track tasks, and monitor project progress from a single platform.

The Solution

Integrated Attendance & Software

The Petal Employee App was designed as a centralized employee management platform that integrates an RFID-based attendance system with a mobile application. Employees scan their RFID cards to record attendance instantly, ensuring accurate and tamper-resistant attendance records.

The mobile application allows employers and employees to view attendance data, apply for leave, and track work progress. This integration reduces administrative overhead while improving transparency and workforce management.

Strategic Choice

RFID-Based Attendance x Mobile App

Context

Organizations were relying on manual attendance registers and disconnected systems, leading to inaccurate records and payroll conflicts.

Rationale

RFID technology enabled fast and reliable attendance capture while preventing manual errors. Pairing it with a mobile application allowed real-time access to data, creating a scalable system.
