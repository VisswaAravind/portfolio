Overview

Trust-first Local Rental Community

Rentsomeware allows users to rent products they need occasionally and rent out their unused products to earn extra money.

By focusing on trust, the platform solves core pain points such as safety, product damage risk, and payment assurance. It aims to make renting a smarter and more sustainable choice than buying.

Product Vision Trusted community-driven marketplace
Target Users Renters & Item Owners
Phase MVP Definition
Problem Statement

Why not just buy it?

Buying products for one-time or occasional use is too expensive. At the same time, many people have products lying unused at home with no easy way to monetize them.

User Pain Points

  • Expensive to buy items for one-time use
  • Fear of product damage or theft
  • No trusted platform to rent items locally

Our Goals

  • Easily find and rent products nearby
  • Rent out unused items and earn income
  • Complete rentals safely with verified users

Strategy

OKRs & MVP Scope

Objective

Enable users to earn side income by renting their unused products.

Key Results

  • List 500+ rentable products within 6 months
  • Onboard 200 active product owners
  • Achieve 100 successful rental transactions
  • Generate ₹2L rental transaction value

MVP Scope (MoSCoW)

Must Have

  • User Authentication & Verification
  • Product Listing System
  • Product Discovery & Booking

Should Have

  • Video-Based Product Verification
  • Rental Tracking Dashboard

Could Have

  • Ratings and reviews
  • Detailed insurance protection

Won't Have (For MVP)

  • Advanced analytics
Launch Readiness

Success Metrics & Learnings

Success Metrics

  • Number of products listed on the platform
  • Active renters and product owners
  • Successful rental transactions
  • User retention rate (repeat rentals)
  • Average rental transaction value

Key Initial Learnings

  • Trust is Paramount: Trust and safety are the most important factors in peer-to-peer rentals.
  • Keep it Simple: Early MVP should strictly focus on core marketplace functionality.
  • Balance is Key: Success depends entirely on balancing supply and demand simultaneously.
