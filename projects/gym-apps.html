Overview

Digitizing Gym Operations

Multi Branded Gym Apps is a mobile application designed to digitize gym operations and improve fitness tracking for gym members. Gym members often struggle to consistently track attendance, workouts, diet plans, and physical progress such as weight and height.

Manual processes made it difficult for both users and gyms to maintain accurate records. This product centralized attendance, workouts, diet plans, and physical metrics into a single platform to simplify tracking and improve gym engagement.

The Solution

A Centralized Fitness Hub

A mobile application was built to digitize gym attendance and fitness tracking. The platform centralized attendance, workouts, diet plans, and physical metrics into one system, reducing manual effort for gyms and making progress tracking easier for users.

The architecture also supports launching multiple gym apps using a shared product foundation, allowing for rapid expansion.

Critical Decision

Single Codebase for Multiple Brands

Context

The business planned to launch similar gym apps for multiple franchises rapidly.

Rationale

  • Faster launch for new gyms
  • Reduced development effort
  • Easier maintenance & Scalable architecture

Team & Execution

Execution & Collaboration

Successfully delivering this product required seamless coordination across a multi-functional team. Stakeholders involved included Designers, Frontend/Backend Developers, QA Testers, and Marketing.

Stakeholders

Design Mobile Dev Backend QA Cloud Marketing

The teams worked in shared sprint cycles with daily standups to coordinate design, development, testing, and release. This ensured that dependencies were managed effectively and the project stayed on track.

Strategy

Release Strategy

Scrumban Approach

A hybrid Scrumban approach was used to deliver incremental releases while supporting continuous improvements. This allowed us to be flexible with changing requirements while maintaining a steady flow of delivery.

Gradual Rollout

Complex features such as workout timers and dashboards were released gradually to reduce risk. This phased approach allowed us to gather user feedback and iterate on features before a full-scale launch.
