Multi-Branded Gym Apps

A centralized mobile solution digitizing gym workflows and tracking for multiple franchises.

🧩 Product Overview

Product Type

Mobile SaaS Platform (B2B2C)

Key Users

Gym members, owners, franchises

Gym members struggled with manual attendance, workout, diet, and progress tracking. This product digitized and centralized these workflows into a seamless mobile experience across different gym brands.

🎯 The Problem

The lack of consistent tracking resulted in poor user engagement and inefficient gym operations. Building separate codebases for each brand was unscalable and costly to maintain.

🧠 Product Decision

Shared Multi-Tenant Architecture

The Decision

Use a single shared codebase for multiple gym brands instead of white-label cloning.

Context

Needed to support multiple gym franchises with similar functionality while maintaining distinct branding and rapid launch cycles.

Rationale

Faster launches, reduced maintenance effort, and scalable growth without adding codebase complexity. Shippable updates reflected across all brands instantly.

📊 MVP & Rationale

MVP Features

  • Member attendance tracking
  • Height & weight tracking
  • User profile management

De-prioritized

  • Advanced diet plans
  • Trainer-led customization

MVP Rationale

Attendance tracking was prioritized to validate core product value early and digitize the most basic user interaction first.

🚀 Outcomes & Impact

Key Outcomes

  • Multi-brand scaling
  • 90% Code reuse

Impact

  • 60% reduction in op-effort
  • Faster franchise onboarding
