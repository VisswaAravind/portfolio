My Product Development Lifecycle

How I go from problem to impact.

1

Problem Discovery

I look for real problems, not just features to build. By analyzing data and talking to users, I validate if a problem is worth solving before writing a single line of code.

2

User Understanding

I define personas and map user journeys to understand the 'why' behind behaviors. I balance user pain points with business goals to build solutions that work for both.

3

Solution Ideation

I explore multiple approaches and weigh trade-offs before committing to a solution. I involve engineering early to assess feasibility and avoid costly changes later.

4

Prioritization & Planning

I use frameworks like RICE to decide what to build first. I focus on high-impact work and define a lean MVP to test ideas without overbuilding.

5

Execution & Collaboration

I collaborate closely with developers and designers to stay aligned. My technical background allows me to communicate clearly and unblock the team quickly.

6

Release & Delivery

I plan phased releases to manage risk and ensure quality. I make sure testing is thorough and stakeholders are prepared for a smooth launch.

7

Measurement & Learning

I define success metrics like retention and conversion to measure impact. I use real data and user feedback to learn what works and iterate for the next cycle.
